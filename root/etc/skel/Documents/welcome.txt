Welcome to ZenithOS!
====================

ZenithOS is a 64-bit operating system written from scratch:
its own UEFI bootloader, its own kernel, its own drivers,
its own compositing desktop and its own applications.

Things to try
-------------
  * Press the Windows key (or click the Z) to open the start menu
    and just start typing to search.
  * Ctrl+Alt+T opens a terminal. Try:  neofetch, bench, cal,
    matrix, fractal, tree /, ls -l /bin
  * Zenith 3D and Mandelbrot render on every CPU core at once.
  * Alt+Tab switches windows, Win+D shows the desktop,
    Print Screen saves a screenshot to ~/Pictures.
  * Drag windows by their title bar, resize them at the edges,
    double-click the title bar to maximize.

Have fun!
